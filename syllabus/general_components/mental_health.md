## Mental health

If you are feeling stressed, worried, or down during the semester, or if you notice signs of emotional distress in someone else, please feel free to reach out to me or whomever you feel comfortable for support.  Willamette's Office of Student Affairs can be a resource; here are some other campus resources: WUTalk, serviced by ProtoCall (24-hour Telephone Crisis Counseling Support) at (503) 375-5353.

If you or someone you know needs to talk to someone right now, text START to 741-741 or call 1- 800-273-TALK (8255) for a free, confidential conversation with a trained counselor 24/7.
