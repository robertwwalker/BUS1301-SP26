# Case 1: The Frozen Screen

**The Scenario:** You are typing an email, and suddenly, the mouse pointer stops moving. You press the "Caps Lock" key on your keyboard, but the little green light doesn't turn on. 

**The Question:** Is this a hardware failure (e.g., your monitor broke) or a software crash (the operating system froze)? How do you know?

**Your Task:** Think about what the "Caps Lock" light represents. Write down your troubleshooting steps before restarting the machine.
